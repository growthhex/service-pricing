📄 Mandatory Verification Documents

Google enforces strict guidelines regarding phone number display on local profiles to combat spam. To run this service, you must supply the following verification documents:

🏛️

Business Registration License

Any valid government-issued registration showing the exact business name and contact number. Accepted documents include:

  • MSME Registration (Udyam)
  • GST Certification
  • Shop & Establishment License
  • Government Company Registration License
⚠️ Must contain the identical business phone number and business name.
🌐

Active Business Website

A live, indexable business website that prominently displays the same business name, address, and matching mobile number in the footer, contact, or header sections.

  • Domain name matching business brand
  • Clear contact page containing details
  • Indexed and accessible by Google's crawlers
⚠️ Free blog subdomains or social media links are not accepted by Google as website entities.
